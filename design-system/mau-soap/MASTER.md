# MAU-SOAP Design System

The canonical, machine-readable design specification is [../../DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

## Working summary

- **Direction:** Academic Wayfinding—an institutional university service, not a generic SaaS dashboard.
- **Palette:** University navy, MAU gold, cool paper neutrals, and restrained semantic states.
- **Typography:** Archivo throughout, with compressed, high-confidence display scale and compact uppercase wayfinding labels.
- **Layout:** Full-width page bands, a 90rem working canvas, ledger summaries, course-first groupings, and responsive single-column reflow.
- **Surfaces:** Flat white records divided by rules. Shadows are exceptional, not structural.
- **Shapes:** Square major regions and 3–8px corners for controls and compact cards.

Do not restore the superseded Plus Jakarta Sans, glassmorphism, floating-card, blue-gradient, or universal hover-lift rules. Refer to `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` before adding a page or component.
